🐦 PIGEON COOP PANIC

Keep the pigeons under control!

LEVELS

HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Goal

Pigeons keep arriving in the waiting area. Put every pigeon into a coop before PANIC reaches 100%.

🖱 Controls

  • Click a pigeon, then click a coop.
  • Wrong move: the pigeon stays put, you get a message.
  • Click a pigeon inside a coop to send it back to the waiting area (+3 panic, combo resets, its points are taken back).

🐦 Pigeon types

  • Normal: any ordinary coop.
  • Social 💬: needs another pigeon already in the coop.
  • Angry 💢: needs an empty coop and nobody may join it afterwards.
  • VIP 👑: only the golden coop.
  • Twins 👯: one pair that takes 2 spots in the same coop.

🏠 Coops

  • Each coop shows used / capacity. You cannot overfill it.
  • The golden coop is reserved for VIPs; nobody else can enter.
  • A closed coop (🚧) cannot be used until it reopens.

😱 Panic

  • Rises steadily while pigeons wait (more waiting = faster; later levels are faster). A full waiting area (7) adds extra.
  • Wrong move: +10. Placing a pigeon correctly: -4. Food Drop: -15.

⭐ Score, combo, stars

  • Correct placement = 10 × combo points. Combo goes up by 1 each time (max x8).
  • A wrong move or sending a pigeon back resets combo to x1.
  • Stars depend on panic left: over 65% = 3, over 35% = 2, otherwise 1.

⚡ Power-ups (per level)

  • Feather Freeze (1): panic stops for 6 s.
  • Extra Coop (1): adds an ordinary coop with capacity 3.
  • Math Hint (2): shows the Pigeonhole minimum for all pigeons and your current coops.

🎲 Random events (from level 3)

Every 18-28 s: Pigeon Rush (2 extra arrive), Coop Closed (6 s), Food Drop (panic -15) or Chaos (panic rises 50% faster for 8 s).

👑 Boss levels (5, 10, 15, 20)

  • 5 Coop Overlord: zero spare spots in the coops.
  • 10 The Falcon: every 12 s a coop closes for 5 s.
  • 15 Pigeon King: extra VIPs, and waiting VIPs raise panic twice as fast.
  • 20 Final Panic: falcon (every 16 s, 4 s closure) + impatient VIPs.

📐 Math, quizzes, Math Lab

  • Pigeonhole Principle: n pigeons in k coops means some coop holds at least ⌈n/k⌉. Coop capacity in each level is built from it.
  • SHOW WHY (level complete screen) shows the steps and a proof by contradiction.
  • Boss levels end with a required 3-question quiz. Other levels offer one optional practice question (+50 per correct answer).
  • Math Lab (main menu): type any n and k and see the calculation.
